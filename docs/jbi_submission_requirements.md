# Journal of Biomedical Informatics Submission Requirements

Source checked: 2026-10-01  
Official guide:
https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/journal-of-biomedical-informatics/publish/guide-for-authors  
Submission system:
https://www.editorialmanager.com/jbi/

## Best article type for the current manuscript

Submit as **Special Communication**, not Original Research.

JBI describes Original Research as presentation of a system or approach that
required the development of innovative methods rather than application of
established techniques. Special Communication is intended for generalizable
lessons, recommendations and novel conclusions from applying existing
biomedical informatics methods.

The manuscript is a methodological/reporting audit and claim framework. It
should therefore be positioned as a Special Communication. Methodological
Reviews are invitation-only.

## Scope requirements

- Focus on a real-world biomedical or clinical problem.
- Explain the generalizable methodological contribution.
- Compare the approach with the current state of the art.
- Avoid presenting a routine application of established methods.
- Translational bioinformatics papers must address human health or disease.

## Required manuscript components

1. Cover Letter
2. Graphical Abstract
3. Statement of Significance
4. Declaration of Competing Interest
5. Title Page
6. Structured Abstract and Keywords
7. Main manuscript
8. Figures and tables
9. Supplementary material, if applicable

## Formatting limits

- Structured abstract headings: Objective, Methods, Results, Conclusion.
- Abstract maximum: 300 words.
- Main body: Introduction, Related Work, Methods, Results, Discussion and
  Conclusion.
- Main body maximum: 6,000 words.
- Combined figures and tables: maximum 8.
- Number main sections and subsections, for example 1, 1.1, 1.1.1.
- The abstract is not included in section numbering.
- The Conclusion must state the applicability of the research.
- No glossary is needed for JBI.
- Use editable Word/LATEX source. PDF is not acceptable as the source file.
- Word manuscripts must use a single-column layout.

## Statement of Significance

Place a table at the end of the Introduction with these four headings:

| Problem or Issue | What is Already Known | What this Paper Adds | Who would Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|

Maximum total length: 150 words.

## Graphical abstract

- Required at submission.
- Dimensions: 531 x 1328 pixels (height x width) or proportionally larger.
- Must be readable at 5 x 13 cm.
- Preferred formats: TIFF, EPS, PDF or Microsoft Office files.
- Submit as a separate file.
- AI-assisted graphical abstract creation must follow Elsevier AI policy.

## Keywords

- 1 to 7 English keywords.
- Avoid multi-word phrases using "and" or "of".
- Recommended JBI-compatible keywords for this paper:
  - reproducibility
  - transcriptomics
  - bioinformatics
  - gene-sets
  - intervertebral-disc

## Cover letter

- Keep under approximately one page.
- State the study aim and main findings.
- Explain fit with JBI Aims and Scope.
- Explain novelty and broader implications.
- Do not include funding information, author declarations, or suggested or
  opposed reviewers.

## Ethics and declarations

- Complete the Elsevier declaration of competing interests tool.
- Upload the generated Word declaration file at submission.
- Disclose funding sources in the standard funding statement.
- Declare any generative AI or AI-assisted use in manuscript preparation.
- Provide CRediT author contribution roles.

## Data and code

- JBI follows Elsevier research-data Option C.
- Deposit research data in a relevant repository.
- Cite and link the dataset in the article.
- If sharing is impossible, provide a reason in the data statement.
- A data availability statement is required at submission.
- GitHub is among the accepted data/code repositories.

## References

- Cite references in square brackets, for example `[3,6]`.
- Number references in order of first appearance.
- Every in-text reference must appear in the reference list, and vice versa.
- Include DOIs where available.
- Reference format need not be strict at submission if it is consistent.
- Final JBI style will be applied during production.

## Artwork

- Cite all figures in the text.
- Number figures in order.
- Submit each image separately.
- Photographs: minimum 300 dpi.
- Bitmapped line drawings: minimum 1,000 dpi.
- Do not combine unrelated images or graphs into one file.

## Peer review

- Single-anonymized review.
- At least two independent reviewers are typically used.
